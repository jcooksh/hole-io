Level 1

    Gulp City

    You are a hole. Swallow the town. Then everything else.

    • Move the mouse to steer, or use WASD or the arrow keys. Drag anywhere on the city to steer. Lift your finger to stop.
    • Anything smaller than you falls in. Each bite fills the yellow ring; a full ring is a level and a bigger hole.
    • Eat the city and you move up a size: the countryside, the Earth, the solar system, the galaxy and beyond.
    • No clock. A hole of a higher level can swallow you, and then it is over.
